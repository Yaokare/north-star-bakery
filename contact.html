Order Notes

Pre-orders are available for pickup only. We do not currently offer delivery. Please allow the following lead times when placing your order:

  • Breads and pastries: 24 hours notice
  • Custom cakes: 48 hours minimum notice
  • Event and catering orders: 5 to 7 days notice

We will confirm your order by email within one business day of receiving your request.
