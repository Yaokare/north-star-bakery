About North Star Bakery

We are a neighborhood bakery rooted in community, craft, and the belief that good bread brings people together. Here is our story.

Our Story

North Star Bakery was founded in 2011 by lifelong Minneapolis residents Clara and James Okafor. What started as a weekend farmers market booth quickly grew into a beloved neighborhood fixture on Maple Street, where the smell of fresh bread has greeted the community every morning for over a decade.

Clara learned to bake from her grandmother in rural Minnesota, where bread was made by hand every week without fail. James brought his background in hospitality and a deep love of feeding people well. Together they built a bakery that feels less like a business and more like a gathering place.

Today North Star Bakery serves hundreds of neighbors, commuters, families, and event planners every week. We still mix our doughs by hand, still use the same sourdough starter Clara has kept alive since 2009, and still believe that the best ingredient in any loaf is time.

How We Source Our Ingredients

We believe great baking starts long before the oven. Our sourcing philosophy is simple: use the best ingredients we can find, as close to home as possible.

Local Grains

We source our wheat and rye flours from Sunrise Mills in central Minnesota, a family-owned mill that has been stone-grinding grain for three generations. Their flour gives our breads a depth of flavor you simply cannot get from commodity flour.

Regional Dairy

Our butter, cream, and eggs come from Clover Creek Farm just outside of St. Paul. Their pasture-raised hens and grass-fed cows produce dairy with a richness that makes a noticeable difference in every croissant and cake we bake.

Seasonal Produce

From late spring through early fall we source fresh fruit directly from vendors at the Minneapolis Farmers Market. Our seasonal pastry flavors change weekly based on what looks best at the market that morning.

Local Honey

The wildflower honey in our Honey Oat Loaf and several of our seasonal pastries comes from Northwoods Apiaries, a small beekeeping operation based in Stillwater, Minnesota. Their honey has a floral complexity that we love.

Meet the Team

North Star Bakery is powered by a small, dedicated team who show up before dawn every day because they genuinely love what they do.

Clara Okafor

Head Baker and Co-Founder

Clara oversees all bread production and recipe development. She has been baking professionally for over 20 years and still gets excited about a perfectly proofed loaf. Her sourdough is the heart of everything we do.

James Okafor

Co-Founder and Operations

James manages the day-to-day operations of the bakery, from supplier relationships to customer experience. His background in hospitality means every visit to North Star feels warm and personal.

Maya Torres

Pastry Chef

Maya joined the team in 2018 and brought her classical pastry training and love of seasonal flavors with her. She leads all pastry and cake production and is the creative force behind our rotating seasonal specials.

Theo Brennan

Baker

Theo has been with North Star since 2020 and handles our focaccia, rye, and specialty loaf production. He is also the person most likely to be found experimenting with new grain blends on his days off.
